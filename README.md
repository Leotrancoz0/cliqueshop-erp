## The product

E-commerce operations rarely arrive through one clean channel. Sales, settlement releases, costs, listings, distributed inventory, returns, advertising, and operational tasks all evolve at different speeds. CliqueShop ERP normalizes those events and places each piece of information next to the decision it supports.

The workspace is designed to:

- surface exceptions and next actions before reports;
- keep cash flow and accrual views conceptually separate;
- track inventory quantity, cost, origin, and location;
- connect sales, listings, advertising, ranking, and commercial viability;
- follow post-sale cases through their financial and physical effects;
- automate eligible scenarios while preserving uncertain cases for human review.

## Marketplace context

This case study includes workflows designed around **Mercado Livre** — known as **Mercado Libre** across Spanish-speaking Latin America — because the ERP was built for a LATAM e-commerce operation.

Marketplace screens reflect regional seller operations: settlement cycles, listings, advertising, claims, returns, shipping effects, and inventory reconciliation. The interface and screenshots remain in Brazilian Portuguese intentionally, as evidence of the actual operating context rather than a generic translated mock.

For readers outside Latin America, Mercado Livre should be understood as a regional commerce adapter. The underlying ERP capabilities — financial reconciliation, inventory costing, distributed stock, profitability analysis, post-sale workflows, automation, permissions, and auditability — apply across marketplaces and regions.

## Three decisions that organize the system

### What needs attention now?

The workspace starts with exceptions: upcoming tasks, alerts, settlement releases, inventory at risk, and events requiring validation. Metrics remain accessible without displacing the next action.

### Is this purchase, price, or listing viable?

The viability workflow combines essential inputs and presents comparable commercial scenarios. Coefficients shown here are demonstrative and do not reproduce production fees or internal pricing parameters.

### What is the complete effect of a return?

Post-sale workflows connect the commercial case, recovery, logistics, and inventory outcome. In eligible regression scenarios, reconciliation controls prevent a second entry of the same returned item. Damaged products or incomplete evidence remain under human review.

                    ↓
       Integration and normalization
                    ↓
   Finance and inventory domain rules
                    ↓
      Protected transactional store
                    ↓
 Authenticated workspace + alerts + routines

The diagram is deliberately abstract. Endpoints, topology, payload contracts, deduplication keys, and proprietary rules are outside the public boundary. See [Architecture and public limits](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Technology at a glance

- **Application:** Python and Django.
- **Persistence:** PostgreSQL.
- **Interface:** responsive JavaScript and CSS.
- **Operations:** containers, scheduled processes, alerts, and platform APIs.
- **Validation:** regression scenarios for finance, cost, inventory, synchronization, post-sale work, and permissions.

Production test counts, volumes, savings, and financial outcomes are not published without authorized, reproducible measurement.

## Product principles

- **Next action first.** Complexity appears only when it becomes useful.
- **Minimal manual input.** Known context is reused instead of requested again.
- **One responsibility per action.** Every visible control produces a real effect.
- **Bounded automation.** Uncertain cases remain visible for human judgment.
- **Defense in depth.** Relevant restrictions also belong to the server and API.

## Run the showcase

The public showcase is static. It uses no database, cookies, local storage, or network calls; navigation state exists only in the local URL fragment.

```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```

Open `http://localhost:8080`, or open `index.html` directly.
